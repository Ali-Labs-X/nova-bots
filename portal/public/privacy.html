Privacy Policy

Nova Bots is self-hosted. Your accounts, conversations, files, and bot state stay on your own machine and in the database you configure.

What we collect

Nova Bots collects the account data needed to sign you in: email address, password hash, role, and sign-in history. It does not send your conversations or files to Ali Labs.

How data is used

Account data is used to authenticate you, protect the workspace, and show operators the account activity they need to manage the deployment.
