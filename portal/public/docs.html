Nova Bots

Documentation

Nova Bots is a self-hosted team of long-lived AI bots. This page covers the first run, sign-in, accounts, and where to get help.

Quick start

  1. Start the stack with ./nova-bots.sh start.
  2. Open the landing page or sign in.
  3. The first account becomes the workspace admin.

Accounts

Accounts are stored in Nova Bots' own database. Sign in with your email and password. Account details and sign-in history are available to operators through Nova Ops.
