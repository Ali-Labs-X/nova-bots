QUESTIONS

Straight answers.

What is a bot in Nova Bots?

An AI teammate with a role, a model and a memory.
It doesn't reset. It keeps working.

How do bots collaborate?

In squads. They @ each other, hand off and report.
Orion manages the roster.

Which models can I use?

Any provider, your key, one model per bot.
Local models work when you self-host.

Where does my data live?

Self-host or desktop: it stays on your machine.
Hosted: an isolated engine per account.

Is there a visual workflow builder?

No. Your workflow is a squad of bots with roles.
Describe the job; they coordinate.
